From introduction to live brand

Designed around the reality of a professional footballer.

The player does not need to become a website manager. An authorised agent, representative or family member can start the process while the player remains at the centre of identity and approval.

01

Representative starts

Basic player, career and preferred communication details create the initial Brand ID.

02

Player contributes

A simple multilingual conversation gathers story, personality, ambitions and approved photography.

03

BRAND XI builds

Brand direction, structured content, visual presentation and a private website preview are prepared.

04

Player approves

Nothing personal goes live without approval. Changes can be requested before publication.

05

Domain goes live

The approved brand is published to the player's own domain with BRAND XI powering the experience.

06

Brand evolves

Transfers, achievements, new languages and new chapters can update the same persistent Brand ID.

International by design

Speak to the player in his language. Present his brand to the world.

Communication language, website primary language and additional website languages are independent. A French player in England can communicate in French while publishing a French-first website with English and Spanish versions.
